# Pediatric emergency guidance integration

Emergency Mode now displays a dated, source-linked guidance panel reviewed on **15 August 2026**.

| Region | Guidance | Publication / status | How it is used |
|---|---|---|---|
| Global | Surviving Sepsis Campaign International Guidelines for Children 2026 | Published 23 March 2026; current 2026 global pediatric sepsis reference | Linked to pediatric septic-shock algorithms and Emergency Mode guidance cards |
| Global | AHA/AAP CPR, BLS, and PALS 2025 | Published 22 October 2025; current resuscitation reference during 2026 | Linked to PALS emergency algorithms and Emergency Mode resuscitation references |
| Nepal | National Guideline on Acute Respiratory Distress Syndrome | Government of Nepal / MoHP document dated 2021; revision status must be verified | Linked to the pediatric ARDS algorithm as a Nepal national reference with an explicit stale-content warning |
| Nepal | NEPAS emergency-care and standardization priorities | 2026 professional-society update, not a treatment guideline | Shown as contextual professional-society information; the app does not treat it as a protocol |

The Nepal-specific search did not identify a newly published 2026 national pediatric emergency treatment guideline in an authoritative open source. Therefore, the app does **not** label the 2021 Nepal ARDS document as a current 2026 guideline. It clearly shows the document date and revision-status warning, and it links to NEPAS’s 2026 priorities and newsletter update about emergency triage, standard treatment guidelines, pediatric drug formulary development, and resuscitation training capacity.

The application shows concise summaries and source links rather than reproducing full copyrighted guidelines. Every emergency workflow retains the requirement to verify the current local hospital protocol, patient-specific data, medication concentration, clinical indication, and senior consultation pathway.
