# Pediatric updates research notes

## Nepal

1. **UNICEF Nepal — Imagining Nepal as a Country of 100 People**. Published 05 May 2026. The brief draws on Nepal Multiple Indicator Cluster Survey 2024–25 findings from the National Statistics Office in partnership with UNICEF and frames inequalities and opportunities affecting children, with recommendations for equitable investment and rural health access. Source: https://www.unicef.org/nepal/press-releases/unicef-nepal-launches-imagining-nepal-country-100-people-highlight-inequalities

## Global

2. **WHO — New global research agenda to strengthen paediatric clinical trials**. Published 06 November 2025. WHO reports a technical agenda for children aged 0–9 years, identifying 172 priorities from 653 research questions, with emphasis on infectious disease, noncommunicable disease, newborn health, early childhood development, nutrition, inclusion, regional collaboration, and research capacity. Source: https://www.who.int/news/item/06-11-2025-who-publishes-new-global-research-agenda-to-strengthen-paediatric-clinical-trials
