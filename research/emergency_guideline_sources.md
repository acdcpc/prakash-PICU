# Emergency guideline research notes

## Global

1. **Surviving Sepsis Campaign International Guidelines for Children 2026**. SCCM page published 23 March 2026. The page states the guideline covers children with sepsis or septic shock, was developed by 68 international experts representing 13 organizations plus six methodologists, and contains 61 statements: five strong recommendations, 24 conditional recommendations, and ten good-practice statements. It reports 20 new recommendations, 13 updated recommendations, and a newer pediatric sepsis definition involving infection with life-threatening organ dysfunction across respiratory, cardiovascular, coagulation, and neurologic systems. Source: https://www.sccm.org/clinical-resources/guidelines/guidelines/surviving-sepsis-campaign-international-guidelines-for-the-management-of-sepsis-and-septic-shock-in

2. **2025 AHA Guidelines for CPR & ECC, used in 2026**. AHA’s CPR/ECC page states that the official 2025 guidelines were published online in Circulation on 22 October 2025 and are based on the most current comprehensive review of resuscitation science, systems, protocols, and education. Source: https://cpr.heart.org/en/resuscitation-science/cpr-and-ecc-guidelines

## Implementation principle

Only dated, source-linked emergency guidance will be shown in the application. The app will summarize scope and update status rather than reproduce copyrighted full guidelines or present summaries as local protocols. The emergency workflow will continue to require local protocol verification and senior escalation.
